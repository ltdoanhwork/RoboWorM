Confusion · Geometry (machine → human)

Resolved human label only: two raters agree, or a checker decides. Rows = machine, columns = human.

Confusion · Hallucinate video (band → human)

Machine band: high → Bad, medium → Medium, low → Good. Human video score = mean of its segment flags (good 0, medium 1, bad 2) rounded back, counted only once every segment of the video is resolved.

Confusion · Hallucinate segment (violated → human)

The per-segment machine signal is binary: violated = motion ratio ≥ 1 against the real-data p95 threshold. Rows = machine, columns = resolved human score.
